Where HVAC companies lose jobs online

01

The emergency search goes to someone else

No AC in a heat wave is a same-hour decision. If your site loads slow on a phone or buries the number, the homeowner calls the next result. Most HVAC sites make people hunt for the phone number.

02

AI recommends your competitor by name

Homeowners now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI who to call. Those engines read structured pages and clear facts. A site built as one long homepage gives them nothing to cite, so they cite someone else.

03

One page for every service

AC repair, furnace replacement, heat pumps, duct work, maintenance plans. If they all live on one generic services page, you rank for none of them and your maintenance plan, the recurring revenue, stays invisible.

What every HVAC build includes

  • A dedicated page for each money service: repair, install, heat pumps, IAQ, maintenance plans
  • Schema markup (HVACBusiness, Service, FAQPage) so search and AI engines can read the business
  • Persistent tap-to-call on mobile and a three field lead form
  • Seasonal offer sections you can swap without a developer
  • Speed tuned for phones: the site loads before the visitor gives up
  • Sitemap submitted to Google and Bing, the index ChatGPT search runs on
