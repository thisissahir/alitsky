Where painting companies lose bids online

01

You are always one of three quotes

Painting is the most comparison shopped trade there is. While the homeowner waits for bids, they study websites. A thin site tells them your bid should be the cheap one. A strong site lets you win without being cheapest.

02

No proof of finish quality

Paint is bought on the finish. If the site has no sharp before and after gallery, the homeowner cannot see the difference between you and the guy with a ladder and a Facebook page.

03

Cost questions answered by competitors

How much to paint a house interior is asked millions of times, in Google and in AI chats. The painter whose site answers it honestly, with ranges and what drives them, earns the trust and the click. Silence sends them elsewhere.
