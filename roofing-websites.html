Where roofing contractors lose jobs online

01

Storm week rewards the fastest site

After hail, every roofer in the county is competing for the same searches. The site that loads instantly, shows proof, and makes contact one tap wins the inspection slot. The slow site funds someone else's crew.

02

A replacement is a five figure trust decision

Homeowners research a roof replacement for weeks and they are terrified of storm chasers. No local proof, no named crew, no straight process explanation, no call.

03

Insurance questions with no answers

Will insurance cover it is the number one roofing question online, in Google and in AI chats. A clear claims guidance page gets read, cited, and remembered. Almost no contractor has one.
